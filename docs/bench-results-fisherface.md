## Protocol

Strict leave-one-out: for each probe the PCA→LDA model is **retrained** on the other n−1 crops (Gram-matrix PCA reduction to n−C directions, then at most C−1 Fisher directions from the whitened between/within scatter matrices; crop size varies per row). Pair distances are directed (probe vs gallery member; one observation per unordered pair); the gallery endpoint is a training point, so FAR/FRR below is mildly optimistic compared with a probe on unseen data. **LOO rank-1 is the primary metric** — there the probe itself is never in the model. Configurations rank by LOO rank-1, then margin.
